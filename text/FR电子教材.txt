时间过的真快，从接触ACCA到现在，已十年有余。


转眼间已从十八岁的无知少年成为一名讲师。还记得我考试的那两年，每天必带BPP练习册，讲义，

计算器和一本厚厚的手写A4纸笔记，穿梭在学校，图书馆，自习室，就连跟闺蜜约场电影，也是背

着沉甸甸的包去赴约，也曾在自习室里哭的像个傻逼，崩溃过后，继续刷题。当时也不知是哪来的

拼劲，不知道前路是何样，也向往着白领精英的生活，但好像离我都特别遥远，只知道先做好眼下

的工作，尽早考完所有的学科，早日脱离这苦海。


我一直不是个特别聪明的学生，但我很用心，是只打不死的小强。老师在课堂上全英文授课的时

候，我大多听不懂，一开始是因为语言问题，后来好不容易适应了英文，又越学越难，有一种在泥

里行走的感觉，摸不到边际，看不到尽头。但我很奇怪，一遇到难点，就会异常兴奋，喜欢这种挑

战，更喜欢征服过后的满足感。随着ACCA越学越难，我也越学越入迷，终于，两年全部考完。


在这所有的科目里，最有感情的就是财务报告，因为太过沉迷于报表，也顺带喜欢准则，后来慢慢

爱上了IFRS。


当你在一个安静的午后，泡一杯咖啡，坐在图书馆窗边的角落里，阳光洒落在笔记上，思考着某条

准则背后的原因时，突然感觉整个人就通透了，这种沉淀式的学习，让我更加迷恋财务报告这门学

科。


你不得不承认，兴趣是最好的老师，就连我现在教课，也常常告诉自己，不要丢掉对报表的喜爱。

因为喜欢，所以投入，准则是一套优美的体系，希望大家也能从中感受到他的力量。

                                         第二章 概念框架
1. Conceptual framework
A conceptual framework is the theoretical basis for determining which events should be accounted for, how
they should be measured and how they should be communicated to the user.The Conceptual Framework is
not a Standard. Nothing in it overrides any requirement in a Standard.
《概念框架》不是国际会计准则，因此不对任何特定的计量和列报确立具体标准，但它是会计准则

的制定基础，它包含了编制报表的基本原则，财报信息的质量特征，会计要素的定义，确认和计量

等。理事会认为，在为数不多的情况下，本《概念框架》和某项国际会计准则之间可能会有抵触，

在此情况下应当以国际会计准则而不是本《概念框架》要求为准。


    考点：IFRS overrides Conceptual framework. 当准则与本概念框架冲突时，以具体准则为主，准则

    凌驾于概念框架。


1.1 Advantages of a conceptual framework
① Accounting standards are developed on the same theoretical principles which avoids a haphazard
    approach to settling standards 会计准则是基于相同的理论原则制定的，避免了随意制定准则的做法

② The development of accounting standards is less subject to political pressure 会计准则的制定受政治压

    力的影响较小

③ Accounting standards use a consistent approach（without a conceptual framework，some standards

    may concentrate on profit or loss whereas some may concentrate on the valuation of net assets）会计准

    则采用一致的方法（没有概念框架，一些准则可能侧重于损益，而另一些准则可能侧重于净资产

    估值）

④ A principle-based approach avoids the need for large volumes of rules to address every scenario 基于原

    则的方法避免了需要大量准则来解决每种情况的问题


1.2 Disadvantages of a conceptual framework
① It is not certain that a single conceptual framework can be devised which will suit all users 尚不确定能

    否设计出一个适合所有用户的单一概念框架
② Given the diversity of user requirements, there may be a need for a variety of accounting standards, each
    produced a different purpose and with different concepts as a basis 鉴于用户需求的多样性，可能需要

    各种会计准则，每种准则都有不同的目的，并以不同的概念为基础
③ It is not clear that a conceptual framework makes the task of preparing and then implementing standards
    any easier than without a framework 尚不清楚概念框架是否比没有框架更容易制定和实施

2. Basic concepts
2.1 Going Concern【Underlying assumption】
The Conceptual Framework states that financial statements are normally prepared on the assumption that the
reporting entity will continue for the foreseeable future. In other words, it is assumed the entity will not enter
liquidation, or cease to trade. 在可预见的未来企业没有破产清算的打算，可预见未来指未来十二个

月。

If this is not the case then the financial statements would be prepared on a break-up basis. 一旦企业失去可

持续经营，就需要用破产清算原则重新编制报表。




2.2 Accrual basis
The effects of transactions and other events are recognised when they occur (and not as cash is received or
paid) and they are recorded in the accounting records and reported in the financial statements of the periods
to which they relate.
The accrual basis gives rise to the "matching" concept – that expenses are recognised based on a direct
association between costs incurred and earning of income.
权责发生制要求企业在收入和费用发生时确认，而不是按照现金收付来确认。同时，在利润表中确

认的收入和费用需要遵循匹配性原则即 matching rule。

Advantages of the historical cost：

① Simple to understand 便于理解

② Amounts are reliable, they can always be verified, they exist on invoice and documents. 容易验证，历

   史成本即购买价值，一般都会有对应的原始凭证

③ Amounts used are objective, as it is more difficult to manipulate cost-based figures. 较为客观，难篡改


Disadvantages of the historical cost:
① The carrying value of assets is often substantially different to market value 跟现值存在较大差异

② No account is taken of inflation meaning that profits are overstated and assets understated 没有考虑通货

   膨胀等因素对科目价值的影响，容易造成资产低估以及利润高估（当资产低估，折旧低估，所以

   会造成利润高估）

③ Ratios like Return on capital employed（ROCE）are distorted 会导致个别财务比例失真


Advantages of using current value：
① Current value is a useful guide for management in deciding whether to hold or sell assets and when to
   settle the liability. 利于管理层决定资产是继续持有还是直接卖掉，以及负债是否现在偿还

IAS 16 Property, plant and equipment


1. Definition and recognition
1.1 Definition
Property, plant and equipment are tangible assets that：

• are held for use in the production or supply of goods or services, for administrative purposes，and

• are expected to be used during more than one accounting period


1.2 Recognition
Items of property, plant, and equipment should be recognized as assets when it is probable that:
① It is probable that the future economic benefits associated with the asset will flow to the entity
② The cost of the asset can be measured reliably


2. Initial measurement
IAS 16 requires PPE initially measured at cost.


Cost is all necessary costs involved in bringing the asset into working condition including cost of purchase,
directly attributable cost etc.

IAS 38 Intangible assets

1. Definition
1.1 Definition
An intangible asset is an identifiable non-monetary asset without physical substance.


1.2 Identifiable：

• “Separable” 可分离的，即能够从主体中分离或划分出来，并能单独或与相关资产一起使用，可以

  出售，转移，租赁或交换，不论主体是否有意进行这些交易，或者；

• Arises from contractual or other legal rights 源自合同或其他法定权利


2. Recognition
① Identifiable
② Controlled assets
③ It is probable that the expected economic benefits that are attributable to the asset will flow to the entity
④ The cost of the asset can be measured reliably


3. Accounting treatment


3.1 External purchased
The recognition criteria are always presumed to have been met for intangible assets that are acquired
separately or acquired as part of a business combination. Therefore can be recognized as intangible asset in
the statement of financial position.


3.2 Internal generated
Internally generated brands, mastheads, publishing titles, customer lists and similar items cannot be
recognized as intangible assets. Expenditure on these times should be recognized as expense when its incurred

IAS 40 Investment property

1. Definition and recognition


1.1 Definition
Investment property is property held to earn rentals or for capital appreciation or both


    投资性房地产常见例子：

• Land held for long-term capital appreciation
• Land held for a currently undetermined future use 没有决定用途的土地属于投资性房地产
• A building that is vacant and is held to be leased out
• Property that is being constructed for future use as investment property

    以下不属于投资性房地产：

• Property intended for sale in the ordinary course of business
• Property being constructed on behalf of third parties
• Owner-occupied property including property held for future use as owner-occupied property


1.2 Recognition
① it is probable that the future economic benefits that are associated with the investment property will flow
    to the entity
② The cost of the investment property can be measured reliably.



2. Measurement


2.1 Initial measurement
Investment property is measured initially at cost, including:
- Purchase price
- Property transfer tax
- Transaction cost
- Professional fee

2. Impairment test / review


2.1 Impairment test【背过】
An asset has been impaired if its carrying amount exceeds its recoverable amount. Recoverable amount is the
greater of fair value less cost to sell and Value in use（future cash flows generated by the asset discounted at

interest rate which allows for the risks specific to the asset ）


    考点：减值损失应确认在当期损益SPL中，属于经营活动分类，除非按照另一准则《IAS 16》资

    产在过去以重估法计量，且产生重估盈余，那么减值损失应作为重估盈余抵减处理。

    如果recoverable amount大于carrying amount 则资产没有减值，无需任何处理

3. Cash-generating unit


3.1 Definition【背过】
CGU is the smallest identifiable group of assets for which independent cash flows can be identified and
measured


    考点：It may not be possible to estimate the recoverable amount of an individual asset. Any entity must

    therefore determine the recoverable amount of the CGU to which the asset belongs. 如果资产存在减值

    所属的现金产出单元的可回收金额。




3.2 Allocation of impairment losses for a CGU
The impairment loss is allocated to reduce the carrying amount of the assets of the unit in the following
order：
① to any assets that are obviously damaged or destroyed
② to any goodwill

【解释】

4. Reversal of previous impairment loss
An impairment loss recognised for an asset in prior years should be recovered only if there has been a change
in the estimate used to determine the asset’s recoverable amount.


The asset cannot be revalued to a carrying amount that is higher than what it would have been the asset had
not been impaired originally, ie its depreciated historical cost had the impairment not taken place.

• Financial liability (the debt)

1. Five-step approach 五步法



           Identify the contract with customers 确认合同的存在：

           ① The contract has been approved by the parties to the contract; 合同各方已批准（通过

  Step 1       合同各方能够识别与拟转让商品或服务相关的权利

           ③ Payment terms for the goods or services can be identified; 能够识别付款金额

           ④ The contract has commercial substance; 合同具有商业实质
           ⑤ It is probable that the consideration in exchange for the goods or services will be
               collected. 很有可能收到客户支付的对价

1.3 Retrospective adjustment 追溯调整
The entity shall adjust the opening balance of each affected component of equity for the earliest prior period
and the other comparative amounts disclosed for each prior period as if the new accounting policy had
always been applied.




2. Accounting estimate


2.1 Definition
Transaction or events with uncertainties inherent would need professional judgment. These professional
judgements are accounting estimates.


 Example:
 1. Allowance for receivables (2% or 3%)
 2. Depreciation method
 3. PPE useful life
 4. PPE residual value
 5. Amount of provision




2.2 Changes in accounting estimates and prospective adjustment
The effect of a change in an accounting estimate shall be recognized prospectively by including it in profit or
loss in:
- The period of the change, if the change affects that period only; or
- The period of the change and future periods, if the change affects both

IAS 10 Events after reporting period


    IAS 10 是高频考点，需要背过定义以及常见例子


1. Definition 【背过】

Event after the reporting period：those events, both favorable or unfavorable, that occur between the end of
the reporting period and the date that the financial statements are authorized for issue.




2. Adjusting events 【背过】
Provide evidence of conditions that existed at the end of the reporting period.


Examples:
① The resolution after the reporting date of a court case giving rise to a liability
② Discovery of fraud or errors
③ The bankruptcy of a customer indicates that their debt was irrecoverable at the reporting date
④ Determination after the year end of the sale or purchase price of assets sold or purchased before the year
    end
⑤ The sale of inventory at less than cost indicates that it should have been valued at NRV




3. Non-adjusting events【背过】
Are those that are indicative of conditions that arose after the reporting period.


Examples:
① Business combinations
② Major restructuring
③ Changes in foreign exchange rates and tax rate
④ Commencing major litigation
⑤ A purchase or sale of a non-current asset
⑥ The destruction of assets due to fire or flood
⑦ The announcement of plans to discontinue an operation
⑧ An issue of shares
⑨ Dividend declared or paid


2.2 Disclosure
Non-adjusting events should disclose the nature and financial effect of the event.

IFRS 13 Fair value measurement

1.    Definition【背过】
Fair value is the price that would be received to sell an asset or paid to transfer a liability in an orderly
transaction between market participants at the measurement date.



2. Valuation techniques


Where fair value is determined using a valuation technique, IFRS 13 prescribes that the technique should be
one of the following：
① Market approach: uses price and other relevant market information for identical or comparable assets
② Income approach: converts future amounts to a single discounted present value amount
③ Cost approach: amount that would currently be required to replace the service capacity of the asset




3. Level of FV【背过】


根据计算FV输入数据 “input” 的可靠程度，可以对FV的结果进行以下排序，排序越高越可靠：

                      Quoted prices in active markets for identical assets or liability that the entity can
     Level 1 Inputs
                      access at the measurement date.
                      Inputs other than quoted prices included in Level 1 that are directly or indirectly
                      observable：
     Level 2 Inputs
                      • Quoted price of identical asset or liability in inactive market
                      • Quoted price of similar asset or liability in active market
                      Inputs for the asset or liability that are not based on observable market data
     Level 3 Inputs
                      (unobservable inputs).
